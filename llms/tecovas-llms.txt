## About

Tecovas makes handcrafted Western boots and accessories from full-grain leathers, exotic skins, and genuine leather soles. All boots are crafted in Leon, Mexico by skilled artisans. The brand sells direct-to-consumer online and in retail stores across the United States, cutting out traditional markups to offer high-quality Western wear at accessible prices.
